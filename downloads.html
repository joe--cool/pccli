Download pccli

pccli is an unofficial Planning Center command line tool, not built, endorsed, or supported by Planning Center.

Choose the archive for your operating system and CPU. Extract it and put pccli (or pccli.exe) in a directory on your PATH. See installation for setup, or release history for older versions and their files.

Verify your download

Download the SHA-256 checksums. Calculate your downloaded archive’s SHA-256 and compare it with the line bearing the same filename in that file before extracting it.

Newer Intel/AMD PCs generally use amd64; Apple Silicon Macs and other ARM64 systems use arm64. Choose based on the machine where pccli will run.
